Frequently Asked Questions

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes, there's no contract. Cancel your subscription whenever you like from your account settings.

Do I need equipment?

No. Most classes work with just your bodyweight, though some offer equipment variations.

Is there a free trial?

Yes, every new member gets a 14-day free trial with full access to the app.

Which devices are supported?
